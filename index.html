PocketSwap — Cozy Local Item Trade-Off Game & 0% Cash Neighborhood Barter App

PocketSwap (https://pocket-swap.onrender.com) is a free, gamified local item barter web app and PWA where neighbors trade real-life items like a Cozy RPG—with 0% cash and zero fees.

Why Traders Use PocketSwap (vs. Buy Nothing, Bunz & Facebook Marketplace)

  • 20-Slot RPG Pocket Inventory: Curate up to 20 real-life items with photos, year made, brand, rarity tier, and condition grade.
  • 12 Local Swap Categories: Gaming, Gadgets, Collectibles, Fashion, Books & Art, Home & Cozy, Music & Audio, Sports & Outdoors, Toys & Board Games, Crafts & DIY, Baby & Kids, and Tools & Garden.
  • Dual Local Radar: Switch between the Cozy Island Game Radar and the Real OpenStreetMap GPS Street Radar with verified daytime public Safe Meetup Zones.
  • Trade Arena & Fairness Appraiser: Offer 1-for-1 or multi-item bundle swaps with automatic fairness scoring, counter-offers, and a Village Wanted (ISO) Board.
  • 4-Digit Meetup PIN & Karma Passport: Complete in-person swaps with a 4-digit handshake PIN or QR code to transfer item ownership and earn 5-star Karma reviews.
